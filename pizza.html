Pizza

Description

This is a classic homemade pizza with a crispy crust and fresh toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. Rollout the dough.
  3. spread sauce and add toppings.
  4. Bake for 12-15 minutes.
