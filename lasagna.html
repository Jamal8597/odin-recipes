Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is the ultimate comfort food. It features layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella chesses

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 pound mozzarella cheese
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Brown ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Add marinara sauce and crushed tomatoes. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Mix ricotta, egg, and garlic in a small bowl.
  6. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture in a baking dish.
  7. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top.
  8. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
